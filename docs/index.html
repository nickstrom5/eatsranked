How it works

  1. 1

    We read the public record.

    Health inspections, business licenses, published honors and open map data.

  2. 2

    We add inspections and check.

    Where we use health inspection records, the app shows the official result, or a grade of our own that is labeled as ours, never as official. Our guides to local favorites are checked by hand.

  3. 3

    You find your place.

    Search, map and save restaurants. Free, with no ads and no account.

Sources differ by state: official inspection and license records where a state or city publishes them, Overture open map data for the rest, and places we check by hand (e.g. Wisconsin fish fries and supper clubs). Grades of our own are labeled as ours; official results are shown as published.

Questions

  • Are the apps and websites free?

    Yes. Every app and website is free, with no ads and no account.

  • Can I download the apps?

    Not yet. The iPhone and iPad apps are coming soon to the App Store. Each state’s website, with search on the web, works today.

  • Where does the data come from?

    Sources differ by state: official inspection and license records where a state or city publishes them, Overture open map data for the rest, and places we check by hand (e.g. Wisconsin fish fries and supper clubs). Grades of our own are labeled as ours; official results are shown as published.

  • Are the grades official?

    Only where we say so. Where a city, county or state publishes an official inspection result, the app shows it as published. Where an app shows a grade of its own, it’s labeled as ours, never as official.

  • Which state is next?

    More states are on the way. Tell us which one you want next.

  • Who makes Eats Ranked?

    Eats Ranked is an independent project by Nicholas Soderstrom, not affiliated with any restaurant or government agency. Questions or corrections: nick@eatsranked.com.
