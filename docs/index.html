How it works

  1. 1

    We read the public record.

    Health inspections, business licenses, published honors and open map data.

  2. 2

    We grade and check.

    Where a city publishes inspections, those records become our own grades and rankings, not official ones. Our guides to local favorites are checked by hand.

  3. 3

    You find your place.

    Search, map and save restaurants. Free, with no ads and no account.

Sources differ by state: official inspection and license records where a state or city publishes them, Overture open map data for the rest, and places we check by hand (e.g. Wisconsin fish fries and supper clubs). Grades are ours, never official.
